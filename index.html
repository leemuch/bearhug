Explore Taipei

Step Out and Discover Taipei

Nestled near Ximending, you're steps away from great food, shopping, history, and the MRT — making all of Taipei surprisingly easy to explore.

Nearby Attractions

🚇

Ximen MRT Station

~5 min walk

One of Taipei's most convenient metro hubs — a quick hop to all major sights.

🛍

Ximending Shopping District

~3 min walk

Taipei's most iconic youth district — shopping, street food, cinema, and street culture all in one.

🏮

Longshan Temple

~5 min by MRT

One of Taipei's oldest and most revered temples — a window into traditional culture and local faith.

🏛

Chiang Kai-shek Memorial Hall

~10 min by MRT

A landmark worth visiting for its sweeping grounds and iconic architecture.

🌃

Ningxia Night Market

~10 min by taxi

A concentrated showcase of Taiwanese street food classics — the perfect night market experience.

🚄

Taipei Main Station

~5 min by MRT

HSR, TRA, MRT, and the Airport Express all converge here — connecting you anywhere with ease.

✈️

Taoyuan International Airport

~50 min by Airport MRT

A smooth, direct ride from the airport straight to your doorstep — the ideal Taipei base.
